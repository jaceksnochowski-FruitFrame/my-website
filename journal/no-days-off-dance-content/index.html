No Days Off: one light, a dancer and a sleeping toddler

October 22, 2026
Jacek Snochowski
Branded ContentBehind the Scenes

A short dance piece with Matt Szczerek, a simple setup at Fade Street Studios, and the music turned down so we wouldn’t wake my daughter.

My two-year-old daughter was asleep on the sofa while Matt and I shot this. We even lowered the music so we wouldn’t wake her. I wanted to keep things simple and work quickly—this was one of those days when being a dad and being a cinematographer happened in the same room.

The finished piece

View the post shared by No Days Off on Instagram

A small idea worth trying

I was already creating content for No Days Off Water, but this particular piece was my idea. Their story and what they stand for resonated with me. Coming from dance, I could see a possible connection between that energy and the dance world.

Matt Szczerek immediately came to mind. He’s a dancer, choreographer and theatre maker based in Dublin, originally from Poland. I knew his movement and felt he would be right for it.

I wanted to make a short piece with Matt and the can of water: something small that could spark a bigger idea or a conversation with the brand.

One light, and the music turned down

We used our third-floor space at Fade Street Studios. The room was completely dark, and I used one strong, hard light as a backlight. That was the lighting setup.

Keeping it simple suited both the idea and the day. With my daughter asleep under a blanket nearby, we kept the shoot quick and the music low. Between being a dad, a cinematographer and co-running the studio, I wanted to find a little time to make something personal—an idea that had been in my head and that I was excited to try with Matt.

Behind the scenes → the result

A short look around the studio on my phone, followed by the complete finished piece.

Trying the Ronin 4D

I also wanted to test my new DJI Ronin 4D and see how it would fit this kind of dance content. It worked really well for the piece. I handled the filming, editing and colour grading, with a voiceover from No Days Off founder Manning Sumner.

No Days Off has since shared the finished piece on its own Instagram channels, crediting Matt and me. It’s lovely to see a small idea find a place with the brand that inspired it.
