JustSync

Thanks for testing JustSync. It's a Mac app that syncs footage from all your cameras and audio recorders by sound, or by timecode if they were jammed, and gives you a ready timeline for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut. Please use it on real jobs and tell me what breaks.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. Drag JustSync into Applications.
  3. Open JustSync from Applications.
  4. When it asks to access your drives or folders, click Allow. It needs to read your footage. Your files are never changed.
  5. Got a licence key from me? Click Trial at the top of the app and paste it in.

How to use it

  1. Drag in a whole shoot: camera folders, recorder audio, the song if it's a music video.
  2. Click Synchronize. Shot with jammed timecode? Choose Tracks > Sync by > Timecode only first.
  3. Open Export Timeline > Export to Folder, then drag the XML file into your editor. Your timeline is ready.

What I need you to test

  • Real shoots: your normal cameras, recorders and file types.
  • Your editor: does the timeline open and is every clip in the right place?
  • Hard cases: long recordings, many cameras, noisy locations, clips with bad audio.
  • Anything confusing: if you had to guess what a button does, tell me.

Found a problem?

  • Click Report a Problem at the bottom of the app. It opens an email with all the details I need. Just add what you were doing.
  • When a new version is out, an orange Update button appears at the top of the app.
