The trickiest part of filming *Monsters* was working out where the story was going while the project was still happening.

The project unfolded over 12 months. Across multiple shoot days, I followed Matt Szczerek and Alessio Motta as they developed their children’s theatre production during a residency at Draíocht in Blanchardstown and later brought it into schools.

There were rehearsals, performances and children’s reactions to film. But alongside all of that, I had to keep asking: what is the story I’m telling here? I was directing and editing the documentary as well as filming it, so keeping hold of that narrative was the main challenge.

## Bringing it together

We finished with interviews at Draíocht to bring the different stages of the project together. For these, I chose a deliberately dark, high-contrast setup. I wanted more control over the light and a theatrical mood that connected the interviews to the show.

The key was an amaran 300c with a softbox and honeycomb grid to control spill. I kept it warmer on their faces, contrasting it with a cooler, harder backlight from a second amaran 300c. A reflector attachment helped direct that backlight.

The documentary follows the development of the show, from the creative process and rehearsals to performances and young audiences experiencing it. I directed, filmed and edited it through Fruit Frame Media.
