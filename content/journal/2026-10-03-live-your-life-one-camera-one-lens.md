For Akewi’s music video *Live Your Life*, I limited myself to one camera, one lens and one light. We had one day to shoot across several locations in Dublin, so the setup needed to be practical to carry and quick to work with.

The budget was very limited. Keeping the equipment small was a deliberate decision: I wanted to spend our time filming and moving between locations.

## The equipment

I shot on a Sony FX6 with a Sigma 14mm f/1.8 lens and an Aputure 60c light panel. That was the kit I chose to work with throughout the day.

I also directed, edited and colour-graded the video, taking the project from the shoot through to the finished film.

## Several locations, one shooting day

We built the shoot around places we could get access to: the artist’s house, a friend’s barber shop, a friend’s gym, Dublin streets and Tola Vintage in Dublin.

That gave us several different settings, but each move had to fit into the same day. The equipment choices were tied directly to that schedule. A small setup made it easier to move between places and get ready for the next scene.

## Working quickly in public places

The biggest challenge was working in locations that were open to the public. We needed to be quick and efficient, so we started early in the morning.

For me, this was the practical reason behind the restrictions. One camera, one lens and one light gave me a manageable setup for a day with several stops and limited time at each.
