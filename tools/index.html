Mac app · Free 20-day trial

JustSync

Drop in footage from all your cameras and audio recorders. JustSync lines everything up by sound and gives you a ready timeline for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut. No clapper, no timecode, no lining up waveforms by hand.

What it does

  • Syncs by sound: any number of cameras, phones and recorders.
  • Music videos: syncs every take to the song, even when it's quiet in the background.
  • Long takes: splits a clip where playback restarted, so every take lands on the song.
  • Clean timelines: one clip per track, stairs layout, beat markers on the song.
  • Exports: Premiere Pro XML (with multicam), FCPXML for Resolve and Final Cut.

Install

  1. Download and open the file.
  2. Drag JustSync into Applications.
  3. Open JustSync. The first time, macOS may ask you to confirm. The "Read me first" file in the download shows how.
